\# AI Interviewer Platform



An AI-powered interview platform designed to conduct dynamic interviews, evaluate candidate responses, support real-time voice interaction, and provide interviewer decision support.



\## Project Vision



The platform will support:



\- Candidate and interviewer portals

\- Resume and job description analysis

\- Dynamic AI-generated interview questions

\- Text-based interviews

\- Voice-based interviews

\- Speech-to-text and text-to-speech

\- Webcam and screen-sharing support

\- Interview monitoring

\- Automated interview evaluation

\- Candidate performance analytics

\- Cross-interview answer consistency analysis

\- Interviewer decision support



\## Core Differentiating Feature



The platform stores answers from previous AI interviews and allows a human interviewer to validate candidate responses later.



When a human interviewer asks a question that is semantically equivalent to a previous AI interview question, the system can:



1\. Identify the related previous question.

2\. Retrieve the candidate's previous answer.

3\. Compare the previous and current answers.

4\. Identify consistent facts and meaningful differences.

5\. Highlight potential discrepancies or contradictions.

6\. Provide evidence and confidence to assist the interviewer.



The system does not automatically label a candidate as cheating. The final decision remains with the human interviewer.
